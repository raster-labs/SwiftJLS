## Interference, failure and stopping

- Preflight reads source/binary/Consumer pins and runs synthetic analyser controls only; smoke records stay outside the campaign. Baseline binaries are never rebuilt silently. All builds, tests, profilers and audits finish before timing; the developer/auditors remain idle during it.
- Before collection, capture two bounded 10-second instantaneous host CPU/process inventories plus AC/thermal state. Identify actual ongoing compilation, tests, profiling, simulator/VM work or other sustained competing work. Ask its owner to pause if necessary; never kill unrelated processes. Record the real preflight activity rather than inventing a percentage threshold from historical `ps` lifetime averages. Readiness is an explicit reviewed receipt, not AC alone. If a numeric interference trigger is desired, establish its measurement/threshold from this preflight and freeze it before any timing, not afterwards.
- Retain per-round power/thermal/process receipts. The existing runner has **no four-second hold**: sequential processes, immediate fsync receipts, condition snapshots after each 18-process round and at least every ten seconds while a process is pending. Its one-second `communicate(timeout=1)` is a polling timeout, not an inserted delay. Do not add arbitrary delays or claim current duration from historical duration.
- Predeclare stop-on source/hash drift, failed fidelity/size assertion, nonzero exit/missing samples, AC loss, thermal/performance warning, or return of the identified interfering workload. Stop the entire campaign, retain its partial evidence, and label it incomplete; do not retain selected clean rows, retry individual failed processes, or automatically restart. Unrecognised observed interference remains in the record and requires an explicit disposition.
- Final analysis happens once after the complete fixed collection. If any primary cell is unresolved/confirmed, the task remains open; no extension, repeated campaign or waiver is authorised by this plan. A new experiment requires a distinct source correction or documented measurement defect and a new predeclared decision, with old evidence retained. Physical testing, Apple RSS/universal workspace and Milestone5 are separate gates.
